🖼️ Viewer Usage
Launch the PyQt5 3D viewer:

Always show details

make run-viewer

Or directly:

Always show details

python viewer3d.py

Optional: Import programmatically

Always show details

from viewer3d import FieldViewer
viewer = FieldViewer()
viewer.show()
